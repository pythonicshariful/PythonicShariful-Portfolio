FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers, the same ones I give every client before we start.

Will the bot work on the real site — or just in a demo?

I test everything against the live site before I hand it over. If it fails live, it's not finished — a bot is only done when it is verified working live.

Will the bot place orders or spend my money by itself?

No. The final step — actually placing an order — is always guarded, so the bot never spends your money by accident.

What if my idea can't be done?

I check the site first — what it allows, what blocks bots — and tell you honestly if it can be done. If something can't be done reliably, I say so upfront.

What do you need from me to start?

Send me the link and tell me what you need: the site and the fields you want, or the flow the bot should do. I'll tell you honestly what it will take.

What do I get when you deliver?

You get the code, a setup guide and a walkthrough, plus honest notes on what to watch after delivery.

How fast will you reply?

I reply personally — usually within 24 hours — and I will tell you honestly whether your idea is possible.
